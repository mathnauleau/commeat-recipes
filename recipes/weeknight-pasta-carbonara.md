# Weeknight Pasta Carbonara

## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti or rigatoni
- 100g guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 2 large eggs + 1 yolk
- 50g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
- 50g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
- freshly cracked black pepper
- kosher salt

## Steps

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to boil. Cook pasta until just shy of al dente.
2. Meanwhile, cook guanciale in a cold pan over medium heat until fat renders and edges crisp.
3. Whisk eggs, yolk, and both cheeses together. Season generously with pepper.
4. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
5. Off heat, add hot pasta to pan with guanciale. Toss to coat in fat.
6. Pour egg mixture over pasta, adding pasta water a splash at a time, tossing constantly until glossy and saucy.

## Notes

Never add cream. The emulsification of eggs, fat, and starchy water is the sauce.
