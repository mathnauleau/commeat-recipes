# One-Pot Burrito Casserole
**Tags:** Dinner

<img alt="One-Pot Burrito Casserole" src="https://images.ricardocuisine.com/services/recipes/7446.jpg" />

> A one-pot, Mexican-inspired weeknight meal of ground beef, black beans, rice and melted cheddar, served with crispy baked tortilla chips. Serves 6.

## Ingredients of the Casserole
* 1 onion, chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, chopped
* 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
* 3/4 lb (340 g) lean ground beef
* 1/2 cup (100 g) parboiled long-grain rice
* 1 can (19 oz / 540 ml) black beans, rinsed and drained
* 1 can (14 oz / 398 ml) diced tomatoes
* 3/4 cup (180 ml) chicken broth or water
* 2 tsp chopped and seeded jalapeño pepper
* 1 tbsp chili powder
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 2 cups (200 g) grated cheddar cheese
* Salt and pepper

## Ingredients of the Topping
* 6 flour tortillas, quartered
* Chopped cilantro (optional)
* Sour cream (optional)

## Steps
1. Place the oven rack in the middle position and preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
2. In a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat, soften the onion and garlic in the oil. Add the beef, breaking it up, and cook until golden brown.
3. Add the rice, beans, tomatoes, broth, jalapeño and spices. Season with salt and pepper. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
4. Sprinkle with the cheese and bake for 10 minutes, until melted. Let cool for 10 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, spread the tortilla pieces on a baking sheet and bake for 5–8 minutes, until crisp and lightly browned.
6. Garnish with cilantro. Serve with the tortilla chips and sour cream.

## Notes
Prep time: 20 minutes. Cooking time: 35 minutes.

Use a skillet that can go from stovetop to oven.
