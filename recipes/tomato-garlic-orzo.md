# One-pot Tomato & Garlic Orzo

**Tags:** Meal

## Ingredients

* 1 cup orzo
* ½ cup coconut milk or heavy cream
* 1 tbsp chili oil (reduce if you prefer less heat)
* ¾ cup water
* ¼ cup fresh basil
* 1–2 cups cherry tomatoes
* 10 garlic cloves, minced
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* Salt & pepper
* ¼ cup Parmigiano Reggiano

## Steps

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
3. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook until they begin to soften and burst.
4. Stir in the chili oil.
5. Add the orzo and toast it for 1–2 minutes.
6. Pour in the water and bring to a simmer.
7. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the orzo is nearly tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
8. Stir in the coconut milk or heavy cream.
9. Add the Parmigiano Reggiano and mix until creamy.
10. Season with salt and pepper.
11. Fold in the fresh basil just before serving.

## Notes

Norwegian waffles are traditionally served as a snack rather than a breakfast food. They are thinner and crisper than Belgian waffles and are almost always made in a heart-shaped waffle iron. The shape is considered an important part of the experience.
Popular Norwegian toppings include:
* Strawberry jam and sour cream
* Butter and sugar
* Butter and brown cheese (brunost)

Maple syrup is also a surprisingly good accompaniment, though less traditional.
For the best texture, allow the batter to rest before cooking and avoid stacking freshly cooked waffles directly on top of one another, as steam can soften their crisp exterior.
