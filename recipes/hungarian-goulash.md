# Authentic Hungarian Goulash (Gulyás)
**Tags:** Dinner

<img alt="Authentic Hungarian Goulash" src="https://www.daringgourmet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Hungarian-Goulash-17.jpg" />

> Hungary's national dish — a rich, paprika-forward beef soup-stew slow-simmered with onions, carrots, and potatoes. Simple ingredients, extraordinary depth of flavor. Serves 6.

## Ingredients of the Goulash
* 3 tbsp lard (or butter/oil)
* 2 large onions, diced
* 2 lbs (900 g) beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
* 3 tbsp quality Hungarian sweet paprika
* 1 tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* ½ tsp caraway seeds (optional)
* 4 cups beef broth
* 1 bay leaf
* 3 carrots, sliced
* 3 medium potatoes, diced
* Sour cream and crusty bread, to serve

## Steps
1. Melt the lard in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Cook the onions until beginning to brown, 7–10 minutes.
2. Add the beef and cook until just browned on the outside.
3. Remove from heat. Stir in the paprika, salt, pepper, and caraway seeds — paprika scorches easily and turns bitter, so don't add it over direct heat.
4. Add the beef broth and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover and simmer for 40 minutes.
5. Add the carrots and potatoes. Return to a boil, then reduce heat, cover and simmer for another 30–40 minutes until the beef is tender and the vegetables are cooked through.
6. Adjust salt to taste. Remove the bay leaf. Serve with crusty bread and a dollop of sour cream.

## Notes
Quality Hungarian-imported paprika makes all the difference — it's worth seeking out.

Goulash is closer to a hearty soup than a thick stew. Don't expect a heavy, reduced sauce.

Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors deepen overnight.
