# Banana Nut Bread
**Tags:** Snack

<img alt="Banana Nut Bread" src="https://cdn.aistoucuisine.com/assets/74afe3e1-aa2d-43b7-991c-b3ebfa71e7f8/banana-bread.webp?format=webp&quality=75&width=1536" />

> A banana loaf denser than yellow cake but moister than wheat bread: tender, flavorful, and not so dry or sticky that you need a glass of water to eat it. Makes about 10 servings, in a loaf pan of about 5 × 9 in (13 × 23 cm).

## Notes
Use fully ripe bananas, with a heavily speckled peel. Store-bought ones are often underripe: ripen them at room temperature. Ripe bananas can be frozen; thaw them unpeeled on the counter.

Both baking soda (reacts with the bananas' acidity) and baking powder (extra leavening) are used.

Don't overmix after adding the flour, or the bread turns tough.

Keeps 4–5 days wrapped in plastic at room temperature. Serve warm or fully cooled.
