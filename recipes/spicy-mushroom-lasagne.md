# Spicy Mushroom Lasagne
**Tags:** Dinner, Vegetarian, Pasta

<img alt="Spicy Mushroom Lasagne" src="https://ottolenghi.co.uk/cdn/shop/files/Spicy_mushroom_lasagne.jpg?v=1716188132&width=1000" />

> A deeply umami, spicy mushroom ragù layered with pasta and two cheeses. A meatless lasagne that gives any meat ragù a run for its money. Serves six as a main.

## Ingredients of the Ragù
* 750 g chestnut mushrooms, halved
* 500 g oyster mushrooms
* 60 g dried porcini mushrooms
* 30 g dried wild mushrooms
* 2 dried red chillies, roughly chopped (deseeded for less heat)
* 500 ml hot vegetable stock
* 1 onion, peeled and quartered
* 5 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
* 1 carrot, peeled and quartered (90 g)
* 2–3 plum tomatoes, quartered (200 g)
* 75 g tomato paste
* 130 ml double cream
* 135 ml olive oil, plus extra for greasing
* Salt and black pepper

## Ingredients of the Assembly
* 60 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
* 60 g Parmesan, finely grated
* 5 g basil leaves, finely chopped
* 10 g parsley leaves, finely chopped, plus 1 tsp to serve
* 250 g dried lasagne sheets (about 14 sheets)

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 230°C fan.
2. Pulse the chestnut and oyster mushrooms in batches in a food processor until finely chopped. Toss with 3 tbsp oil and 1 tsp salt, spread on a lined baking tray and bake for 30 minutes near the top of the oven, stirring three times, until golden-brown. Set aside. Reduce oven to 200°C fan.
3. Soak the dried mushrooms and chillies in the hot stock for 30 minutes. Strain, keeping the liquid (top up to 340 ml with water if needed). Roughly chop the mushrooms, finely chop the chillies. Set aside separately.
4. Pulse the onion, garlic and carrot until finely chopped. Fry in 60 ml oil over medium-high heat for 8 minutes until golden. Add the pulsed tomatoes, tomato paste, 1½ tsp salt and 1¾ tsp black pepper. Cook 7 minutes. Add the rehydrated mushrooms, chillies and roasted mushrooms. Cook 9 minutes without stirring, then add the reserved stock and 800 ml water. Simmer on medium heat for 25 minutes until ragù consistency. Stir in 100 ml cream and cook 2 more minutes. Remove from heat.
5. Mix both cheeses and both herbs in a bowl. In a round 28 cm (or 30×20 cm rectangular) dish, layer: one-fifth of the ragù, one-fifth of the cheese mix, then lasagne sheets. Repeat three more times, finishing with ragù and cheese — 5 layers of sauce and cheese, 4 layers of pasta.
6. Drizzle with 1 tbsp cream and 1 tbsp oil. Cover with foil and bake 15 minutes. Remove foil, increase to 220°C fan, bake 12 more minutes turning halfway. Grill for a final 2 minutes until edges are crisp. Rest 5 minutes, drizzle with remaining cream and oil, top with parsley and black pepper.

## Notes
Can be made vegan by omitting the cream and cheese.

For a child-friendly version, reduce the black pepper and skip the chilli.

The ragù can be made ahead and refrigerated. The full lasagne can also be assembled the day before and baked once back at room temperature.
