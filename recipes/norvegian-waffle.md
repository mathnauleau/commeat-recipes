# Norwegian Waffles

**Tags:** Brunch

<img alt="Norwegian Waffles" src="https://thestayathomechef.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Easy-Norwegian-Waffles-1200x720.jpg" />


> Thin, crispy, heart-shaped waffles flavored with cardamom are a beloved Norwegian tradition. Served fresh from the waffle iron, they are often enjoyed with jam, sour cream, brown cheese, or simply butter and sugar.

## Ingredients of the Waffle Batter

* 3 eggs
* 50 g sugar
* 550 ml whole milk
* 450 g all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp vanilla sugar
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp ground cardamom
* 100 ml water
* 175 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled

## Steps

1. Melt the butter and set it aside to cool slightly.
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs and sugar until light and slightly frothy.
3. Add the milk and mix until combined.
4. Add the flour, vanilla sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and cardamom.
5. Whisk until the batter is smooth and free of lumps.
6. Gradually add the water, stirring until the batter reaches a smooth consistency. The batter should be neither too thick nor too runny.
7. Stir in the cooled melted butter until fully incorporated.
8. Let the batter rest for 15–30 minutes.
9. Preheat a Norwegian-style heart-shaped waffle iron.
10. Lightly grease the iron with butter if needed.
11. Pour enough batter onto the center of the iron to cover the cooking surface.
12. Close the lid and cook for 2–4 minutes, until the waffle is golden brown and crisp.
13. Carefully remove the waffle and place it on a wire rack while cooking the remaining batter.
14. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

## Notes

Norwegian waffles are traditionally served as a snack rather than a breakfast food. They are thinner and crisper than Belgian waffles and are almost always made in a heart-shaped waffle iron. The shape is considered an important part of the experience.
Popular Norwegian toppings include:
* Strawberry jam and sour cream
* Butter and sugar
* Butter and brown cheese (brunost)

Maple syrup is also a surprisingly good accompaniment, though less traditional.
For the best texture, allow the batter to rest before cooking and avoid stacking freshly cooked waffles directly on top of one another, as steam can soften their crisp exterior.
