# Norwegian Waffles

**Tags:** Brunch

<img alt="Norwegian Waffles" src="https://thestayathomechef.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Easy-Norwegian-Waffles-1200x720.jpg" />


> Soft, heart-shaped waffles flavored with cardamom are a beloved Norwegian tradition. They are typically served with strawberry jam, sour cream, whipped cream, or slices of brown cheese (brunost).

## Ingredients

### Waffle Batter

* 400 ml all-purpose flour
* 100 ml sugar
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tsp ground cardamom
* 400 ml milk
* 3 eggs
* 100 g unsalted butter, melted

### For Serving (Optional)

* Strawberry or raspberry jam
* Sour cream
* Whipped cream
* Butter
* Brown cheese (brunost)

## Steps

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and ground cardamom.
2. Gradually pour in the milk while whisking to create a smooth batter.
3. Add the eggs and whisk until fully incorporated.
4. Stir in the melted butter and mix until the batter is smooth and uniform.
5. Let the batter rest for about 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Preheat a waffle iron. A traditional Norwegian heart-shaped waffle iron is ideal, but any waffle iron will work.
7. Lightly grease the iron if needed.
8. Pour in enough batter to cover the cooking surface and cook until the waffle is golden brown.
9. Transfer the waffle to a cooling rack while preparing the remaining batter.
10. Serve warm with your preferred toppings.

## Notes

Norwegian waffles are thinner and softer than Belgian or American waffles. Cardamom is considered an essential flavor in many traditional recipes and gives the waffles their characteristic Nordic aroma.
For an authentic Norwegian experience, serve the waffles with a spoonful of sour cream and strawberry jam, or with thin slices of brunost (Norwegian brown cheese). Allowing the batter to rest before cooking helps create a softer texture.
