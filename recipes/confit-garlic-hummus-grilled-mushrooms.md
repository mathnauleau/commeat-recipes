# Confit Garlic Hummus with Grilled Mushrooms
**Tags:** Vegetarian

> Creamy hummus infused with slow-roasted confit garlic, topped with charred, marinated mushrooms in a savory-sweet dressing of lemon, thyme, and cascabel chilli. Serves four as a side or as part of a mezze spread.

## Ingredients of the Hummus
* 2 heads of garlic, top fifth cut off to expose the cloves
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 2 tbsp tahini
* 300 g cooked chickpeas (or good-quality jarred chickpeas)
* 2 tbsp lemon juice
* 3 tbsp ice-cold water
* Salt, flaked sea salt, and black pepper

## Ingredients of the Grilled Mushrooms
* 140 g chestnut mushrooms, quartered
* 120 g shiitake mushrooms, roughly torn in half
* 1 garlic clove, crushed with the side of a knife
* 60 ml olive oil
* 1 lemon, skin finely shaved into 3 strips, then juiced to get 2 tbsp
* 5 g thyme sprigs
* 1½ tsp maple syrup
* 1 dried cascabel chilli
* 1 tbsp dill, roughly chopped
* ½ tbsp parsley, finely chopped

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
2. Drizzle the heads of garlic with 1 teaspoon of oil and sprinkle with a little salt and pepper. Wrap tightly in foil and roast for 40 minutes, until the cloves are softened and golden-brown. Remove the foil, and once cool enough to handle, squeeze out the cloves, discarding the papery skins.
3. While the garlic is roasting, heat a large griddle pan on high heat. Add the mushrooms and grill for about 8 minutes, turning throughout, until all sides have dark char marks. You may need to do this in batches.
4. Transfer the grilled mushrooms to a medium bowl with the crushed garlic clove, olive oil, lemon strips and juice, thyme, maple syrup, 1½ tsp flaked salt, and a generous grind of black pepper. Mix well.
5. Add the dried chilli to the griddle pan and cook for 4 minutes until fragrant. Roughly chop the chilli and add it to the mushroom bowl along with its seeds. Set aside to marinate for 1–2 hours.
6. For the hummus, add the roasted garlic cloves and ¾ tsp flaked salt to a food processor along with all remaining hummus ingredients. Blitz until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
7. Spread the hummus in a shallow bowl, creating a large well in the centre with the back of a spoon. Add the dill and parsley to the mushrooms, then spoon the mushroom mixture into the well along with the oil and aromatics.
8. Serve immediately.

## Notes
The hummus will keep for up to 3 days in the fridge, covered with a little oil to prevent a crust from forming.

The mushrooms are best grilled on the day of serving — they become limp and less meaty after time in the fridge.
