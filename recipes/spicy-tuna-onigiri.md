# Spicy Tuna Onigiri
**Tags:** Snackkk

<img alt="Spicy Tuna Onigiri" src="https://www.abeautifulplate.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/spicy-tuna-onigiri-3.jpg" />

> Homemade Japanese rice balls filled with a simple spicy tuna mix of olive oil-packed tuna, mayonnaise, sriracha, rice vinegar and scallion. Makes 5–6 onigiri (4–6 servings).

## Ingredients of the Onigiri
* 1½ cups Japanese short-grain rice (sushi rice)
* 15 fl oz (440 ml) cold water
* Kosher salt, for shaping
* 3 toasted nori sheets, cut crosswise in half
* Toasted black sesame seeds (optional)

## Ingredients of the Spicy Tuna Filling
* 1 can (4.5 oz) albacore tuna in olive oil, drained
* 1½ tbsp mayonnaise
* 1 tsp sriracha
* ½ tsp rice vinegar
* 1 scallion (green parts only), finely sliced

## Steps
1. Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh sieve under cold water until the water runs clear. Drain well.
2. Cook the rice with the water in a rice cooker. Without one: bring to a boil, reduce to low, cover and cook 16–18 minutes until the water is absorbed. Fluff gently, cover and let stand 10 minutes.
3. Mix the tuna, mayonnaise, sriracha and rice vinegar with a fork. Stir in the scallion.
4. Let the rice cool enough to handle. Set out a bowl of room-temperature water and a small bowl of salt.
5. Dampen your hands, then sprinkle both palms lightly with salt.
6. Press about ½ cup of rice into a thick oval in your palm. Make a well, add about 1 tbsp of filling, and close the rice over it.
7. Gently shape into a wide triangle, rotating the ball and keeping it lightly packed so it holds without being compressed.
8. Sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired. Wrap with a nori strip, top with a small spoonful of filling and serve immediately.

## Notes
Prep time: 20 minutes. Cooking time: 30 minutes.

Onigiri rice is seasoned only with salt while shaping, unlike sushi rice, which is seasoned with vinegar and sugar.

A rice cooker gives the most consistent rice. Shaping is the trickiest step: keep hands wet and the rice lightly packed.
